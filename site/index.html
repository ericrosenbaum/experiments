Experiments

Small things built to try out an idea. Each one lives on its own branch of ericrosenbaum/experiments.

  • assemble
    2D molecular self-assembly: charged polygons in a heat bath form rings, chains and lattices.
  • sleep spectrogram
    A browser spectrogram viewer for scanning long overnight audio recordings.
  • Oh My Eyes
    A party game: draw a picture card, roll letter dice, describe it before the timer runs out.
