    About

    I design things that invite people to tinker: to mess around, try ideas and surprise themselves.

    As Director of Scratch Lab at the Scratch Foundation, I explore new creative possibilities for the future of Scratch, the world's largest online coding community for kids.

    I co-invented the Makey Makey invention kit with Jay Silver: a little circuit board that lets you turn anything into a computer key. I also created Beetle Blocks, Singing Fingers, Glowdoodle and other playful tools, and have worked with JoyLabz, Google Creative Lab and the NYU Music Experience Design Lab.

    In 2015 I completed my PhD at the MIT Media Lab in the Lifelong Kindergarten group, with a dissertation called Explorations in Musical Tinkering.
