Spoken Worlds

Make and play little adventures by talking. Your voice, your recordings, all on this device.

      Rules cheat sheet 
      go <place>         move there after the result
needs <thing>      only works if you have it (item or switch)
needs no <thing>   only works if you don't
else <text>        what you hear if a "needs" fails
gives <item>       add to inventory
takes <item>       remove from inventory
sets <switch>      turn a hidden switch on   (clears <switch> turns it off)
sound <name>       play a sound effect before the result
once               option disappears after use
win / lose         end the game

Two options with the same "player says" are variants of one
choice: the first whose needs are met plays (first time / again).

      Settings

      Voices marked “on device” don't use the network.
