Analysis
Open a recording to begin m4a / mp3 / wav / anything the browser can decode. A 30-minute file takes a few seconds. Scroll to zoom the time axis, drag to pan, click to place the playhead, space to listen. Shift-drag measures a stretch of time. Double-click drops a marker.

Quiet stretches

  • Stretches where loudness stays under the threshold appear here. Click one to zoom to it.

The red line on the loudness strip is the threshold. Tune it so normal breaths cross it and the gaps between them do not. This is a scanning aid, not a diagnosis.

Markers

  • Double-click the spectrogram or press M to mark an event. Markers are remembered per file in this browser.

Reading it

Each breath is a broadband smear in the spectrogram and a bump on the loudness strip. Regular breathing looks like a picket fence at a 1 or 5 second zoom. Snoring is a low buzz with harmonics under about 500 Hz. A stretch with no bumps followed by a loud gasp is what you are looking for.
